# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Groups of travelers and trekkers who coordinate trips, share live locations, chat, and compare progress.

## Product Purpose

Provide a live, trusted view of who is where, help groups organize routes and progress together, and make sharing achievements effortless. Success is clear situational awareness, easy coordination, and confidence while moving.

## Brand Personality

Bold, group-first, precise. The experience should feel focused and confident.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a generic maps-only app or a plain Google Maps clone. Avoid sterile, utilitarian mapping UI or decorative glass layers that reduce legibility.

## Design Principles

- Map-first clarity over visual effects.
- Group presence is always obvious at a glance.
- Progress and achievement feel athletic and grounded.
- Actions are direct, quick, and reliable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize strong contrast and readable text sizes. Support reduced motion where possible. We can refine targets later.
